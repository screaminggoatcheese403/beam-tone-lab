ABOUT TONE LAB

All 134 unique Songsterr tab entries are included: 98 from Favorites or playlists plus 36 additional tabs you uploaded. Altogether, 49 of the entries are marked as uploaded by you.

Each entry loads a starting recipe based on one of the original workbook artist references or an approximate genre template. These are not verified reproductions of studio settings; especially your personal tabs and tracks without genre information need review by ear.

Model names come from your BEAM Solo workbook. Your five instruments and pickup configurations are included. Changing a control here does not change the Blackstar app.

Presets save in this browser only. Use Export and Import to transfer them between devices. This version does not call your Cloudflare Worker or any paid AI service.
